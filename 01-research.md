# Research

Summary of the team's research phase: competitive benchmarking, literature review, and market context that shaped the product direction.

## Market context

The global e-learning market surpassed $315 billion in 2021, with a projected CAGR of over 20%, heading toward $1 trillion by 2028 — a fast-growing space where differentiation on learner engagement and personalization matters.

## Literature review

The team screened 50 sources (30 from Google Scholar, 20 from ACM Digital Library), filtered by publication year (2018–2024), and narrowed to 12 included papers after title, abstract, and eligibility review — focused on educational technology and pedagogy research.

Key findings referenced:
- AI/ML and big data can support adaptive learning, dropout prediction, and personalized feedback, though data privacy and security are recurring concerns.
- Real-time analytics and modular platform enhancements (e.g., failure prediction, communication tools) are an active area of development in existing MOOC ecosystems.
- Learner retention remains a common, unresolved issue across MOOC platforms regardless of region.
- Practicality and teacher/learner support are strongly associated with positive online learning interest, based on survey-based studies.

## SWOT summary

- **Strengths:** personalized roadmaps, interactive pop-up quizzes, progress transparency, accessibility features
- **Weaknesses:** real-time features still require internet connectivity; content creation is resource-intensive
- **Opportunities:** institutional partnerships, corporate training, AI-driven personalization
- **Threats:** competition from established platforms (edX, Coursera), market saturation, evolving data privacy regulation
